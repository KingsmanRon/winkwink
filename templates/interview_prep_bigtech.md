# Interview Preparation: Big Tech & Cloud Vendors

## Company Types
- Big Tech: Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Apple, Netflix
- Cloud Vendors: AWS, Azure, GCP, HashiCorp, Snowflake, Datadog
- Growth Tech: Uber, Airbnb, Spotify, Salesforce, Stripe

---

## Technical Preparation

### System Design at Scale
- [ ] Design for billions of users
- [ ] Global load balancing and traffic management
- [ ] Microservices architecture patterns
- [ ] Service mesh (Istio, Envoy, Linkerd)
- [ ] Observability at scale (metrics, logs, traces)
- [ ] Event-driven architectures
- [ ] CQRS and event sourcing

### Cloud-Native Deep Dives
- [ ] Kubernetes internals (scheduler, CNI, CSI)
- [ ] Serverless architectures and cold start optimization
- [ ] Multi-tenancy and isolation patterns
- [ ] Cost optimization strategies
- [ ] FinOps principles
- [ ] Infrastructure as Code best practices
- [ ] GitOps and deployment strategies

### Reliability Engineering
- [ ] SLO/SLI/SLA definitions
- [ ] Error budgets
- [ ] Chaos engineering
- [ ] Incident management
- [ ] Post-incident reviews
- [ ] Capacity planning
- [ ] Performance testing

## Sample Interview Questions

### System Design
1. "Design the infrastructure for a global CDN"
2. "How would you architect a multi-tenant SaaS platform?"
3. "Design a system for processing 1PB of data daily"
4. "Explain your approach to zero-downtime deployments at scale"
5. "Design a disaster recovery strategy for a global service"

### Technical Deep-Dives
1. "Walk me through how you would debug a distributed system failure"
2. "How do you handle database schema migrations at scale?"
3. "Explain the trade-offs between different consistency models"
4. "How would you design a system that handles 10x traffic spikes?"
5. "Design a secrets management system for a large organization"

### Behavioral (STAR Format)
1. "Tell me about a time you led a complex migration project"
2. "Describe an incident you managed and what you learned"
3. "How do you handle disagreements about technical approaches?"
4. "Tell me about a time you had to influence without authority"
5. "Describe a situation where you had to make a difficult trade-off"

## Leadership Preparation

### For Senior+ Roles
- [ ] Leading large-scale migrations
- [ ] Incident response and post-mortems
- [ ] Cross-team collaboration
- [ ] Mentoring junior engineers
- [ ] Driving technical decisions with stakeholders
- [ ] Handling technical debt prioritization
- [ ] Building team processes and culture

### Technical Leadership Signals
- Ownership: Taking end-to-end responsibility
- Influence: Driving decisions beyond your team
- Vision: Thinking about long-term architecture
- Communication: Explaining complex topics clearly
- Mentorship: Growing other engineers

## Questions You Should Ask

### Technical
- "How are architecture decisions made across teams?"
- "What's the typical scope for a senior/staff engineer?"
- "How do you handle technical debt prioritization?"
- "What's the deployment cadence and process?"

### Team & Culture
- "What's the on-call rotation and incident response process?"
- "How do teams collaborate on cross-cutting projects?"
- "What does success look like in this role at 30/60/90 days?"

### Growth
- "What learning and development opportunities exist?"
- "How do you support engineers growing into staff+ roles?"
- "What's the promotion process like?"

## STAR Story Preparation

Prepare 5-7 stories that demonstrate:

1. **Technical Leadership**
   - Led a complex migration or architecture change
   - Made a critical technical decision with trade-offs

2. **Problem Solving**
   - Debugged a difficult production issue
   - Optimized a system for performance/cost

3. **Collaboration**
   - Worked across teams to deliver a project
   - Handled disagreement with a colleague

4. **Impact**
   - Project that significantly improved metrics
   - Initiative that changed how the team works

5. **Growth**
   - Learned a new technology quickly
   - Mentored someone successfully
